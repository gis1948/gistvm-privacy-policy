GisTvm Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

GisTvm ("GisTvm", the "App", "we", "us", or "our") is an iOS application for iPhone and iPad provided by GIS. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, why it handles it, and what happens to it — including when you delete your account.

In short: GisTvm has no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. If you use the App without signing in, nothing is sent to us. If you create a Gis account, your playlists and favourites can be synchronised through the Gis sync service, and you can permanently delete that account and its local data from inside the App.

1. What We Do Not Collect

The App does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not access your advertising identifier, does not use the App Tracking Transparency framework, and does not build a profile of you for advertising purposes.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

2. Using the App Without an Account (Guest Mode)

You can use the App fully without creating an account. In Guest mode the App does not contact our servers. Playlists and favourites you create in Guest mode are stored only on your device, in a separate area that is not tied to any account, and are never uploaded.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Depending on the features you use, the App may store the following on your device:

  • Playlist sources you add — including M3U playlist addresses and, if you use an Xtream Codes provider, the provider server address, username, and password that you enter. These are stored so you do not have to re-enter them.
  • Cached channel and media information retrieved from those sources, including channel names, categories, logos, and playable stream addresses.
  • Favourites — the favourites groups you create and the stream addresses you add to them.
  • Image cache — thumbnails and logos downloaded for display. This is a regenerable cache.
  • Preferences — playback buffer size, language, appearance, device name, and whether automatic synchronisation is enabled. These are device settings and are not associated with your account.

4. Gis Accounts and Synchronisation

A Gis account is optional. It is only required if you want your playlists and favourites to synchronise across your devices.

To create an account you choose a username and a password. We also generate a 12-word recovery phrase. The password is not stored on your device — it is sent to the Gis sync service to authenticate you. The recovery phrase is shown to you once and is not stored on your device — if you lose it, it cannot be recovered by us.

When you sign in, the App stores the following in the device Keychain, which is protected by the operating system and is not backed up to other devices:

  • your session and device authentication tokens;
  • a device key pair generated by the App, used to identify your device;
  • a device identifier for that account;
  • a synchronisation key, stored in encrypted form; and
  • the account's encrypted key-escrow manifest.

5. What Is Sent to the Gis Sync Service

If you enable synchronisation, the App sends the following to the Gis sync service so that your data can be synchronised between your devices:

  • your username, and either your password or your recovery phrase, when you authenticate;
  • your device public key, device name, and device identifier;
  • changes to your playlists — which, for Xtream Codes sources, include the provider server address, username, and password you entered; and
  • changes to your favourite groups and favourite items (stored as stream addresses).

Playlist and favourite payloads are encrypted on your device using AES-256-GCM with a key associated with your account before they are uploaded, and each change is signed. The sync service stores this data so it can return it to your other devices. Your synchronised data is associated with your account and is removed when you delete the account.

6. Third-Party Services That You Choose

If you add a playlist or an Xtream Codes provider, the App contacts that provider directly using the credentials you supply. Those providers operate independently of us, and we do not control their privacy practices. Please review their own privacy policies.

We do not control the privacy practices of any website or streaming service that you choose to access through the App.

7. How Long We Retain Information

Account information, including synchronised playlists and favourites, is retained for as long as your account exists. If you do not create an account, we hold no information about you at all.

We may retain limited information where required by law, necessary for security, or needed to prevent abuse.

8. Deleting Your Account

You can permanently delete your Gis account directly from inside the App: Settings → Account → Delete Account, and then confirming the prompt.

When you confirm, the App first asks the Gis sync service to delete your account. Only if the server confirms the deletion does the App then remove your data from the device. If the deletion cannot be completed — for example if you are offline — nothing is erased and you remain signed in, so that you can try again. The App never reports a deletion that the server did not confirm.

After a confirmed deletion, the App removes:

  • your account on the Gis sync service, including your synchronised playlists and favourites;
  • your playlists and cached channel information stored on this device for that account, including any IPTV provider server address, username, and password you had entered;
  • your favourites stored on this device for that account;
  • the local synchronisation queue for that account; and
  • your account's Keychain material: session and device tokens, device key pair, device identifier, synchronisation key, and escrow manifest.

What we intentionally keep: signing out of your account (as opposed to deleting it) leaves your account and its data intact. After a confirmed deletion, the App also keeps a small number of device-level items that do not belong to your account: your Guest-mode playlists and favourites, your preferences such as language and playback buffer, the regenerable image cache, and a device-level key used to protect local Keychain data. None of these can identify your deleted account.

You can also request deletion by e-mailing us at gis1948@gmail.com. See the Account & Data Deletion page.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information handled through the App, including storing authentication material in the iOS Keychain and encrypting synchronised payloads before transmission. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience IPTV player. It is not directed to children, it is not in the Kids category, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us improperly, please contact us so that we can review it and, where appropriate, delete it.

11. Your Choices

  • Use the App in Guest mode and send us nothing.
  • Turn off synchronisation to stop uploading further changes.
  • Delete individual playlists and favourites from the App at any time.
  • Delete your entire account and its data from the App at any time.
  • Clear the App's image cache from Settings.
