GisTvm Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: October 2, 2026

GisTvm supports permanent in-app account deletion. You do not need to contact us, and you do not need to wait for a manual process.

How to Delete Your Account in the App

  1. Open Settings.
  2. In the Account section, tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm the prompt.

The App then asks the Gis sync service to delete your account. The App only removes local data once the server has confirmed the deletion. If the request fails or you are offline, nothing is erased, you stay signed in, and the App offers a Retry option.

Alternative: Email Us

If you cannot use the App, send a request to gis1948@gmail.com with the subject GisTvm Account Deletion Request, including the username of the account you want removed so we can identify it.

If you have lost your username, we cannot recover it: the account identifier is derived from the username and cannot be reversed. In that case, deletion must be completed from the App while signed in.

What Is Deleted

After the server confirms, the following are removed:

  • your account on the Gis sync service and its synchronised playlists and favourites;
  • playlists and cached channel data stored on the device for that account, including any IPTV provider server address, username, and password you entered;
  • favourites stored on the device for that account;
  • the local synchronisation queue for that account;
  • account Keychain material: session and device tokens, device key pair, device identifier, synchronisation key, and escrow manifest.

What Is Not Deleted

The following are device-level rather than account-level and are intentionally retained. None of them can identify your deleted account:

  • Guest-mode playlists and favourites;
  • preferences such as language, appearance, and playback buffer size;
  • the regenerable image cache;
  • a device-level key used to protect local Keychain data.

Signing Out Is Not Deleting

Sign out only ends the session on this device. Your account and its synchronised data remain, so you can sign back in and keep your playlists and favourites. Use Delete Account to remove the account.
